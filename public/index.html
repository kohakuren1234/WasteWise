WASTE MANAGEMENT PROJECT

One Class, One Planet: Our Waste Management Journey

Waste management is not just a school responsibility! It is a vital commitment to protecting our environment and future. In CVSU - Naic campus, every classroom plays a key role in reducing waste and promoting sustainability. This project showcases the dedicated efforts of our class in managing waste properly, applying responsible practices, and inspiring others to take action. Through education, cooperation, and consistent discipline, we strive to build a cleaner, safer, and greener campus for everyone.

♻️

Recycling

Transform waste into valuable resources by recycling materials properly.

  • Separate paper, plastic, glass, and metal
  • Rinse containers before recycling
  • Check local recycling guidelines
  • Avoid contaminating recyclables
🌱

Composting

Turn organic waste into nutrient-rich soil for gardens and plants.

  • Compost fruit and vegetable scraps
  • Add yard waste and leaves
  • Maintain proper moisture levels
  • Turn compost regularly for aeration
🔄

Waste Reduction

Minimize waste generation through conscious consumption choices.

  • Use reusable bags and containers
  • Buy products with minimal packaging
  • Repair items instead of replacing
  • Donate or sell unwanted items
